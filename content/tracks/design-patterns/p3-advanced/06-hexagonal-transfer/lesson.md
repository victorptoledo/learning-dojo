# Hexagonal Transfer

The phase's patterns in one use case. A transfer service reaches into a global account map, a
2009 rate feed and the console, and loses money when a transfer fails halfway. You will give it
**ports** it owns, move the technology into **adapters** (one of them an **anti-corruption
layer** over the feed), make transfers all or nothing with a **unit of work**, and wire it all
in one **composition root**. The grader also checks the dependency rule by reading your
packages. Press **Enter** to create the kata folder and open it.
