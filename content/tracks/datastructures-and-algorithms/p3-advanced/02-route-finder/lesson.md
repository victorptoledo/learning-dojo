# Route Finder

Build the toolbox of the previous lesson: a union-find with path compression and union by size,
a BFS that measures a maze, Dijkstra's algorithm with a priority queue and lazy deletion, the
shortest path itself, and Kruskal's minimum spanning tree. The hidden tests use a 2,000 × 2,000
maze, a graph of 200,000 nodes and a million edges, and a million union-find operations shaped
to punish a version without the two optimisations. Press **Enter** to create the kata folder and
open it.
