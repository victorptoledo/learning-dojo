# Checkpoint 1: Fundamentals

This checkpoint closes the **Fundamentals** phase. It has two parts, and you need both:

1. **The quiz** (press **Q**): eight questions over the whole phase. You need 7 right.
2. **The integration kata** (press **Enter**): a small inventory that uses structs, an enum,
   `match`, `Vec`, `HashMap` and borrowing together.

Do them in any order. Before you start, review:

| Lesson | Ask yourself |
| --- | --- |
| Variables and Mutability | When do I need `mut`? What does shadowing change? |
| Ownership | What happens to a `String` after I pass it to a function by value? |
| Borrowing | How many `&mut` can exist at once? Can they overlap with `&`? |
| Structs and Enums | When do I use `&self`, `&mut self` and `self`? Is my `match` exhaustive? |
| Collections | `get` or `[i]`? `String` or `&str`? How does `entry` work? |

> [!TIP]
> If a quiz question surprises you, open the lesson it comes from again before retrying. The
> explanation after each answer says which idea it tests.
