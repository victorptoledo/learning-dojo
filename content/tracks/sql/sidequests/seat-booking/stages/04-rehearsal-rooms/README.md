The theatre rents its two rehearsal rooms (`rooms`) to bands, by the hour. Write `rooms.sql`:

| Column of `room_bookings` | Rule |
|---|---|
| `id` | `bigint` generated by the database, primary key |
| `room_id` | required, references `rooms` |
| `customer_id` | required, references `customers` |
| `during` | `tstzrange`, required, never empty; the slot from its start (included) to its end (excluded) |

Two bookings of the same room never overlap, and the table itself refuses them: a direct
`INSERT` of two overlapping bookings fails with an **exclusion violation** (SQLSTATE
`23P01`).

`book_room(p_room integer, p_customer integer, p_from timestamptz, p_to timestamptz)
RETURNS bigint` books `[p_from, p_to)` and returns the booking's id, or `NULL` when it
overlaps a booking of that room. A slot that ends when it starts is refused by a check, and
one that ends before it starts is an error. Back to back is fine: 10:00-12:00 and
12:00-14:00 do not overlap.

**The races** (session A books Studio A from 10:00 to 12:00 and stays open):

- B books Studio A from 11:00 to 13:00: B **waits**; if A commits, B returns `NULL`; if A
  rolls back, B gets the room.
- B books Studio A from 12:00 to 14:00, or Studio B from 10:00 to 12:00: B does not wait.

> [!TIP]
> Stage 1 solved "one active booking per seat" with a unique index; overlaps are the same
> problem with ranges instead of equal values. Look up `EXCLUDE USING gist` and the
> `btree_gist` extension (`CREATE EXTENSION IF NOT EXISTS btree_gist` belongs in your file).
> `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`, without a conflict target, also works with exclusion constraints.
