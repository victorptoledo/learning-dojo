# Checkpoint 1: Fundamentals

This checkpoint closes the **Fundamentals** phase. It has two parts, and you need both:

1. **The quiz** (press **Q**): eight questions over the whole phase. You need 7 right.
2. **The integration kata** (press **Enter**): a sign-up flow that validates every field,
   refuses extra fields, binds its SQL, fixes the new account's role and encodes what it
   shows.

Do them in any order. Before you start, review:

| Lesson | Ask yourself |
| --- | --- |
| OWASP Top 10 | Why is authentication not authorisation? How does SSRF reach the metadata service? |
| Injection | What can never be a bound parameter, and what do you do instead? Why do `%` and `_` matter? |
| Validation and XSS | Why `fullmatch`? Which context needs which encoding? Why is `javascript:` dangerous in `href`? |
| Authentication and access control | Which cookie attributes, and why? What is mass assignment? Why rotate the session id at login? |

> [!TIP]
> If a quiz question surprises you, open the lesson it comes from again before retrying. The
> explanation after each answer says which idea it tests.
