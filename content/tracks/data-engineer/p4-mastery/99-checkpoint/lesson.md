# Checkpoint 4: Final Exam

This checkpoint closes the **Mastery** phase and the whole track. It has two parts, and you
need both:

1. **The quiz** (press **Q**): eight questions over the whole track, weighted towards
   lineage, governance, reliable data products and leading a platform. You need 7 right.
2. **The integration kata** (press **Enter**): the governance and reliability tooling of a
   shop's warehouse. Column-level lineage, personal data that follows it through copies and
   hashes, access that is denied by default with masks and row filters, and the numbers of an
   incident: which products are stale, whom to tell, which runs to backfill, and how much
   error budget is left.

Do them in any order. Before you start, review:

| Lesson | Ask yourself |
| --- | --- |
| Lineage, catalogs and governance | What does column lineage answer that table lineage does not? Is a hashed e-mail personal data? What must an erasure rewrite? |
| Reliable data products | Why is a product only as fresh as its stalest input? In what order do you handle an incident? How many days does a 99% objective allow? |
| Leading a data platform | What must exist before a data mesh works? When do you buy instead of build? |
| Orchestration (Intermediate) | Why must a backfill run downstream tasks, in dependency order, with idempotent tasks? |

> [!TIP]
> If a quiz question surprises you, open the lesson it comes from again before retrying. The
> explanation after each answer says which idea it tests.
