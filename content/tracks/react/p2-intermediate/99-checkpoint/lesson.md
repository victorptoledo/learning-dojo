# Checkpoint 2: Intermediate

This checkpoint closes the **Intermediate** phase. It has two parts, and you need both:

1. **The quiz** (press **Q**): ten questions over the whole phase. You need 85%.
2. **The integration kata** (press **Enter**): a small shop. A typed cart reducer, a cart
   context with a guard hook, a product list that loads, fails and retries without races, and
   a cart summary that edits quantities.

Before you start, review:

| Lesson | Ask yourself |
| --- | --- |
| Custom Hooks | What are the rules of hooks and why? Do two calls of a hook share state? How do you test a hook? |
| Context and useReducer | What does context solve, and what does it cost? Why a guard hook? What makes a reducer pure? |
| Data Fetching | Which states does a request have? How do you avoid races? What does a server-state library add? |
| Routing | What does an outlet do? What belongs in the URL? Why must the server still authorise guarded routes? |

> [!TIP]
> If a quiz question surprises you, open the lesson it comes from again before retrying.
