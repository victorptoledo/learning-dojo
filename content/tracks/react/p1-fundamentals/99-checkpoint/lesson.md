# Checkpoint 1: Fundamentals

This checkpoint closes the **Fundamentals** phase. It has two parts, and you need both:

1. **The quiz** (press **Q**): ten questions over the whole phase. You need 85%.
2. **The integration kata** (press **Enter**): an accessible sign-up form with a pure
   validator, controlled inputs, errors tied to their fields, focus on the first invalid field
   and an async submit with a pending state.

Do them in any order. Before you start, review:

| Lesson | Ask yourself |
| --- | --- |
| JSX and Components | What are the rules of JSX? Why must a render be pure? What is `children`? |
| Props and State | Why is state a snapshot? When do you need an updater? How do you update an array without mutating it? What should be derived instead of stored? |
| Events and Lists | Why must you pass a function to `onClick`? What goes wrong with index keys? Why does `0 && x` show a 0? |
| Effects and Refs | What does an effect synchronise with? When does its cleanup run? Which effects should not exist? When do you reach for a ref? |
| Forms | Controlled or uncontrolled? How do you tie an error to its field for a screen reader? What do actions and `useActionState` add? |

> [!TIP]
> If a quiz question surprises you, open the lesson it comes from again before retrying. The
> explanation after each answer says which idea it tests.
